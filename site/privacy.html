Privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026. This is a draft for version 0.3.0.

What goes through our server

Our signaling server connects two computers. It sees your 9-digit ID, the ID you connect to, and the connection messages needed to set up the link. A password you type when connecting is forwarded to the other computer through the server. The server does not store any of this.

What does not

Screen video and mouse and keyboard input go directly between the two computers over WebRTC, encrypted with DTLS-SRTP. The server never receives them.

What is stored on your computer

Your ID and password are saved in a local settings file on your PC. No account or email is required.

Your control

You approve connections, or share your password only with people you trust. Click New to replace your password at any time.

This page describes the current prototype and will be updated before a public release.
